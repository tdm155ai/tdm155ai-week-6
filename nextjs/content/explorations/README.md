Examples to take inspiration from: the sketches, drafts, exercises, and routines through which painters, composers, poets, dancers, actors, and teachers worked out what they were making and how to get better at it, from Leonardo's horses to a ballet class to Franklin rewriting the *Spectator*. Each case shows the working record where one survives and says what the maker was trying out. The [concepts](/concepts) give the vocabulary for what you see here.

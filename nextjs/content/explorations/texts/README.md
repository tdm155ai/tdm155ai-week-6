title: In their own words

author: claude

---

Sixteen passages in which painters, composers, teachers, actors, and rhetoricians say how they learned or how they taught: Leonardo on the order of study, Cennini on drawing every day and copying one master, Reynolds on sketches and first thoughts, Constable on skying, Franklin rebuilding the *Spectator*, Czerny on scales, Bach's title page for the Inventions, Fux's first lesson, Quintilian and Rainolde on the exercises of rhetoric, Lessing on the actor, Stanislavski on rehearsals, Jaques-Dalcroze on exercises that make automatisms, Feuillet on learning a dance from notation, and Hokusai's postscript at seventy-five.

Each page gives the edition the excerpt comes from, its public-domain status, and a link to the source. They were selected and excerpted for Madeleine's Learning Lab week 6 collection.

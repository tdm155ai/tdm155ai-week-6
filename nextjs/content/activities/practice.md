Choose a skill you'd like to internalize yourself, or, alternatively, help Claude "internalize" it by creating a Claude skill. At least one thing; more if you can. It has to be something that will be valuable for your project.

## The choice

- **Internalize it.** Something you want to learn and have in your own hands and eyes: focusing a lens, lighting a face, placing a mic, cutting a block, writing a scene, keeping a character consistent across shots. You'll build it the way the fields in [Concepts](../concepts/README.md) build it: isolate the part, repeat it with variation, get feedback, put it back into the whole. See [purposeful practice](../concepts/learning/purposeful-practice.md), [decomposition](../concepts/learning/decomposition.md), and [feedback](../concepts/learning/feedback.md).
- **Externalize it.** Something you want to learn well enough to hand off: a job you'd rather Claude did the right way every time, captured as a [skill for Claude](../concepts/learning-with-ai/agent-skill.md), a folder of instructions, examples, and tools. Writing a good one means knowing what good looks like, so this is also learning, just pointed outward. See [What is a Claude skill?](../skills/intro-to-skills.md), [SKILL.md](../concepts/learning-with-ai/skill-md.md), and [instructions vs. training](../concepts/learning-with-ai/instructions-vs-training.md).

Either way, the test is the same: does your project need it, and will you be able to tell, in a week, that something changed?

## What to turn in

- The skill you chose, named in a sentence, and which kind it is (yours or Claude's).
- Why your project needs it.
- For a skill of your own: your studies, the small repeated attempts, with what varied and what you noticed each time. A [practice log](../concepts/learning/practice-log.md) in your own words is enough.
- For a Claude skill: the `SKILL.md` and whatever it needs, run on at least one new case, with a note on what you had to add after the first run (the [study for an agent](../concepts/learning-with-ai/studies-for-an-agent.md)).
- One honest line on the [division of labor](../concepts/learning-with-ai/division-of-labor.md): what you did, what Claude did.

## To fill in

- When it's due and where it goes.
- Whether the second skill, if you choose one, has to be the other kind.

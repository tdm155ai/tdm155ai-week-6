The week is called Studies and Skills, and the day is built to match. Every field that trains people has a small, repeatable piece of work that builds a skill: the painter's study, the musician's étude, the athlete's drill, the actor's rehearsal, the photographer's test. Today you'll do three of them, in three zones, each around one skill you can practice in the room and get better at before you leave it.

**First, a conversation** about [learning skills, and making one for Claude](skills-talk.md): what a skill is (a developed capacity to notice differences and act on them), what practice does, and what changes when a model can do the work. Then the second sense of the word: a skill for Claude, a folder of instructions that teaches it a job, which you can only write well if you know what good looks like.

**Then three rotations**, one skill each:

1. [Focus](rotation-1-focus.md). The camera zone. We're focusing on focus: Lensbabies on the video cameras, prime lenses wide open on the DSLRs, manual and autofocus on every camera, until you can put the plane of sharpness where you mean it.
2. [Block printing, one skill per group](rotation-2-block-printing.md). Trace and transfer, cut, print. Nobody does the whole process; each group receives the last group's work and passes its own on. The subject is the hand-off, which is how work has always been split among people, and is now split between people and AI agents.
3. [AI video and continuity](rotation-3-ai-video.md). Character sheets, props, backgrounds, and the problem of keeping them the same from shot to shot, with the scripts in this repo doing what an app like Higgsfield hides behind its interface.

Each rotation page says what the skill is, what you repeat and what varies, where the feedback comes from, and which [concepts](../concepts/README.md) to read for the vocabulary. The question under all of it is the week's: what are you learning when you do it, and what are you learning when a model does it?

**Finally, [the week's practice](practice.md):** choose at least one skill your project needs, and either internalize it yourself through studies or externalize it by building a skill for Claude.

These pages are outlines, filled in through the day.
